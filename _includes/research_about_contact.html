How I approach the work

Start with the decision.

The useful question is not which dashboard or model to build. It is which decision needs to improve, who owns it and what evidence will help.

Keep the evidence close.

Definitions, assumptions and limitations belong alongside the insight. A convincing explanation is not a substitute for a sound financial foundation.

Design for people.

Technology creates value when it fits the way people work. Clear ownership, human judgment and adoption are part of the design—not an afterthought.
