CFO Decision Flight Recorder

A completed governed decision-intelligence project designed to make recommendations, human decisions, evidence, review and outcomes visible and replayable.

The business question

A dashboard can show what changed without preserving why a decision was made, which evidence supported it or how it was reviewed. That context matters when leaders revisit an outcome.

The approach

The completed public showcase spans eight executive decisions across three fictional business units. It separates model-derived recommendations from authorized human decisions, preserves point-in-time evidence, compares base/upside/downside scenarios, exposes finance and governance checks, and connects decision replay to measured outcomes. The static release uses synthetic data and remains separate from the authenticated Foundry application.

My contribution

Independent finance-domain design, decision-record architecture, executive workflow requirements, governance controls, finance logic, validation and AI-assisted implementation. The work demonstrates how decision evidence and accountable human authority can be carried through the executive experience.

What to explore

Open the command center, inspect a decision record, compare the model-derived recommendation with the authorized human decision, review scenarios and economics, then replay the decision through its evidence, governance events and observed or projected outcome.

Scope & boundaries. The public showcase is read-only. It does not connect to Foundry, invoke an AI model or execute an action. Its data is synthetic. It is a personal portfolio project—not employer production data or a claim that visitors can operate the private application.
