AI Finance Decision Twin

An executive FP&A decision-intelligence prototype that connects uncertainty, scenarios, financial trade-offs, governed signals and accountable human review.

The business question

A point estimate alone cannot communicate the trade-offs between growth, margin, cash and risk. Decision-makers need a way to challenge assumptions and explore alternatives without losing the context.

The approach

The implemented Stage 1 prototype uses synthetic data and four connected workspaces: Executive Pulse, Scenario Lab, Decision Studio and Governance. Global filters and scenario levers update financial outcomes, target-risk views, stress surfaces and decision options in real time. The broader design includes probabilistic forecasting, causal-driver thinking, constraint-aware choices and decision memory.

My contribution

Independent finance concept, scenario and decision-workflow design, requirements, validation and AI-assisted development. Doctoral finance training informs the emphasis on assumptions, uncertainty and disciplined interpretation.

What to explore

Change a scenario assumption, inspect the corresponding financial view and compare the available decision options. Use the governance workspace to understand the intended role of evidence and human review.

Scope & boundaries. Interactive executive prototype, not a production forecasting or autonomous decision system. Advanced causal methods, optimization, closed-loop learning and broader enterprise controls are roadmap concepts—not claims that every capability is implemented or validated.
