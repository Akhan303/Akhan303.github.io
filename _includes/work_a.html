Provanta

A finance intelligence concept that keeps arithmetic and metric meaning governed, while using AI to help explain the results.

The business question

Finance leaders need to move from a performance signal to its drivers and a defensible decision. A plausible narrative is not enough when the underlying figures, assumptions or definitions cannot be reconciled.

The approach

The public demonstration connects executive performance, variance investigation and scenario analysis using synthetic finance and operating data. The concept separates deterministic financial calculation from controlled AI explanation.

My contribution

Independent product work spanning the finance problem, decision journey, requirements, validation and AI-assisted development. The public showcase shares the experience without distributing the private implementation.

What to explore

Start with a financial movement. Investigate the drivers, compare an explicit scenario and inspect the supporting evidence and limitations before drawing a conclusion.

Scope & boundaries. Demonstration and research prototype using synthetic data—not a production SaaS service. No claim of live ERP integrations or certifications. Some product areas represent future direction or are constrained by available evidence. Outputs require qualified human review.
